Rules

All players start with 3 lives. If a player loses all lives, they are inactive and cannot choose cells, but can be revived by finding a Heart.
Blaster (common)
After a correct answer – strikes 1 life.
Dual Blasters
After a correct answer – strikes 2 lives. You may take 2 lives from one player, or 1 life from two different players.
Thermal Bomb (timer)
You lose 1 life yourself.
Starbase Explosion (rarest)
Each player on your team loses 1 life.
Stellar Heart (rare)
Gain +1 life yourself or gift it to someone. If you have 3 lives, you may gain a 4th.
